Feeding Five Thousand

Privacy Policy

Feeding 5K Profile operates Feeding Five Thousand, a group meal planning app.

Information we collect and use

People using the app can enter event and meal details, guest names, age groups, dietary requests, and food sign-up information. We use this information to display and coordinate event and meal plans.

Event and guest information, including dietary requests, is publicly accessible and editable. Do not enter information you do not want others to see.

If SMS notifications are enabled and you choose to subscribe, we collect your phone number, notification choices, verification and consent records, and information about messages and opt-outs. We use these to verify your number, send your selected updates, manage your subscription, and honor opt-out requests.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use service providers, including Supabase for data storage, Vercel for hosting, and Twilio for SMS and phone verification when SMS is enabled. These providers process information as needed to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

Storage and requests

Event information and SMS records may be retained in databases, service-provider records, and backups. Contact us with questions or requests concerning your information at rgoodwel@gmail.com.
